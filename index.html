Robot hand motion study

This short study takes about 10 minutes. Each video shows a person handling an object with both hands (top) and two robots, A and B, repeating the same motion (bottom).

Please watch each video to the end and choose the robot whose hands handle the object most like the person's hands. There are no right or wrong answers; go with your impression.

Taking part is voluntary and anonymous. We do not collect your name or email, and you may stop at any time. Your answers will be used, in aggregate, in a research paper. A computer or tablet screen works best.
